The vessel depicts a face with fangs and an intimidating expression, associated with Aiapaec as the most powerful deity in Moche culture. The significance of the face is because Aiapaec is religiously significant in Moche culture. Since Aiapaec is the most powerful deity in the culture, him being depicted on this vessel demonstrates religious/ceremonial significance. It is widely believed that vessels depicting Aiapaec were used to contain beverages like corn beer, the liquid being poured ceremoniously from the energy of Aiapaec.
