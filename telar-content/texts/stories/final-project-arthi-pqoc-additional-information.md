title: "Additional Information"
---

Evidence of Moche ceramics dates back to around 100 CE to 800 CE. The region's geography heavily shaped its emergence, as river valleys provided fertile land in an otherwise arid coastal environment. Over time, their influence expanded up and down the coast with Moche art styles appearing in a range of locations, though scholars debate whether this represented a unified state or a collection of related regional states sharing a common artistic and religious tradition. The vessel being examined is dated to the Early Intermediate Period.
